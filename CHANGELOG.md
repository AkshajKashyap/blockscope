# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-11

Initial alpha release.

### Included

- Provider-independent Ethereum block, transaction, receipt, and log normalization.
- Uniswap V2-compatible event decoding, metadata lookup, and reserve reconstruction.
- Conservative strict sandwich-candidate detection and observed pair economics.
- Canonical-provenance fixed-input Uniswap V2 counterfactual mathematics.
- Anvil-backed observed replay and independent front-omission EVM experiments.
- Optional observed-cycle balance attribution and local trace reconciliation.
- Golden block `17000000` live verification and a deterministic eight-block evaluation artifact.

### Alpha limitations

- Ethereum mainnet and a narrow Uniswap V2-compatible execution surface only.
- Candidates are structural evidence, not confirmed attacks or claims of intent.
- No wallet-level victim loss, attacker profit, USD valuation, or population-level evaluation.
- Replay fidelity depends on Anvil capabilities and archive-provider state, tracing, and quotas.
- The checked-in corpus is small and not representative.
- No project license has been selected yet.
